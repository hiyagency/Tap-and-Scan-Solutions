# NFC.HIY product context

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian business owners, professionals and creators choosing NFC cards and keychains to share social profiles, review destinations and business links. Mobile buyers need to identify a suitable product and understand configuration before checkout.

## Product Purpose

The existing Next.js storefront sells configurable physical NFC products. The catalogue, Supabase customer authentication, cart, logo uploads, shipping quotes and owner CRM already exist and must remain intact. Success means clearer product discovery and less purchase uncertainty, not fabricated urgency or unverified conversion claims.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, tangible. The user-confirmed September 2026 redesign brief calls for a premium technology-product presentation, near-black foundation and sampled logo-yellow actions.

## Anti-references

Generic dropshipping layouts, neon gradients, fake reviews or visitor counts, invented delivery promises, excessive glass, endless identical card sections and oversized mobile headings.

## Design Principles

- Show real product assets before decorative abstractions.
- Make the next purchase action obvious.
- Put factual stock, compatibility and shipping context close to decisions.
- Preserve business logic and separate unavailable information from confirmed claims.
- Keep owner operations fast and independent of storefront motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic controls, 44px touch targets and readable layouts from 320px through large desktop. No color-only status communication.
